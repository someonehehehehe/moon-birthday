01 / a letter from my heart to yours

Some feelings are too special
not to put into words.

✦　·　✧　·　✦

To my Jaanu,

Today is your day, Jaanu, and if there is one thing I wish you could feel through this screen, it is just how loved you are.

Distance means I cannot be there to give you a birthday hug, see your smile up close, or make a thousand little memories beside you today. But it will never mean that you are any less close to my heart.

I hope this new year of your life brings you soft mornings, reasons to laugh, courage for the hard days, and all the little things your heart has been quietly wishing for. You deserve a love that feels safe, a life that feels kind, and happiness that stays.

Until I can celebrate beside you, let this little universe remind you that somewhere, under this same sky, someone is thinking of you with the biggest smile.

No matter the distance, always yours,

Chaand ♡
